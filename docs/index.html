04

About

I've spent 9+ years designing frontend architecture for complex web applications, enterprise systems and product platforms — mostly on Vue.js and Nuxt.

My approach is layered architecture: separating business logic, UI and the data layer so products stay maintainable as they scale. I've taken products from zero to MVP and carried them into production, and led migrations across Vue 2 → 3, Nuxt 2 → 3 → 4, and PHP → Nuxt 4.

Beyond the framework, I work across the wider web platform — TypeScript, browser APIs, WebSocket, SSR, and backend integration through REST, JSON-RPC and gRPC. I test what I build, with Playwright, Vitest, Cypress and Jest.

Outside of work I follow AI and developer tooling closely — Cursor, Claude Code, local LLMs, n8n — and maintain an open-source Nuxt module for audio analysis.
